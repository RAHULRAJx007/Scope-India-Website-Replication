Students who successfully complete their courses get the opportunity to work on live projects alongside experienced developers for a duration of 3 months under Suffix E Solutions. This is the OJT (On the Job Training) programme.

SCOPE INDIA provides a Course cum Internship Certificate upon successful completion of the training and OJT. This certificate is issued under Suffix E Solutions and serves as a recognised experience certificate for your resume.
