Who We Are

The Story Behind SCOPE INDIA

SCOPE INDIA — Center for Software, Networking & Cloud Education — was founded in 2007 with a singular purpose: to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world IT employment in Kerala.

Backed by Suffix E Solutions Pvt. Ltd., an active IT company, our training programmes are designed and delivered by professionals who work in the industry every day. This gives our students an unmatched advantage — they learn tools, workflows, and practices that are live in production environments.

Over 18 years, we have expanded to four strategic locations across South India, launched 10+ specialised courses, and placed over 1,000 students per year into top IT companies.

Our Mission

SCOPE INDIA seeks to close the knowledge gap between education and employment by offering interactive, industry-focused IT training led by proficient working professionals aligned with the latest trends and technologies. Our objective is to provide people with the technical expertise and confidence they need to build fulfilling careers.

  • ✔ Industry-aligned curriculum
  • ✔ Practical skill development
  • ✔ Career-focused training

Our Vision

Our Journey

18 Years of Milestones

2007

Founded in Thiruvananthapuram

SCOPE INDIA opens its first centre at Technopark, Kerala — offering software and networking courses to fresh graduates.

2010

Suffix E Solutions Partnership

Formally backed by Suffix E Solutions Pvt. Ltd., bringing real-world industry trainers directly into the classroom.

2013

2,000 Alumni Milestone

Crossed 2,000 placed students and expanded course offerings to include cloud computing and server administration.

2016

Thampanoor Centre Opens

Second centre launched in Thampanoor, Thiruvananthapuram — bringing training closer to city students.

2019

Kochi & Nagercoil Expansion

Expanded to Kochi, Kerala and Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu — extending our reach across South India.

2021

Online Learning Launched

Full online training platform launched during the pandemic — enabling students from anywhere to access Scope India courses.

2023

AI & Data Science Course

Launched a comprehensive 180-day Data Science & AI programme with live internship, meeting industry demand head-on.

2025

15,000+ Alumni & Growing

SCOPE INDIA surpasses 15,000 placed alumni, cementing its position as Kerala's premier IT training institute.
